    Meet Om, editor in the making

    A first-year B.Tech Computer Science student at VIT Pune who has just started learning video editing. A curious mind, a creative perspective, and an appreciation for technology, art and meaningful experiences.

    I am drawn to cinematic storytelling, visual composition and filmmaking, and I come to editing from Indian classical music and the flute, which I play as a hobby. So I listen for rhythm before I look at pictures.

    Honest status. I have no showreel, client work or published edits yet. I would rather say so than dress up practice as achievement. This page grows as my work does.

    How a brief would go

    Because I am new, I will tell you honestly what I can do in the time you have.

    1. BriefYou tell me what the video is for, how long it should be, and the deadline.
    2. AssemblyI sort the footage and build a rough cut that tells the story.
    3. PolishPace, colour, sound and titles, with your feedback on the cut.
    4. DeliveryAn export in the format you need, plus a thumbnail if you want one.
